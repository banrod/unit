## Public-safe abstractions

### Identity is contextual coherence

IAM can be introduced as the product layer that keeps identity, intention, memory, permissions, and policy coherent while work moves through changing tools and execution contexts. Identity is therefore more than a name: it is a maintained relationship among purpose, boundaries, history, and permitted action.

### Substrate and self are distinct

The execution substrate should not contain product identity or organizational doctrine. Product intention is translated through an adapter into typed graphs and explicit capability grants. This supplies a teachable distinction between the one who intends, the policy that constrains, the interface that translates, and the substrate that executes.

### Capability is granted, not assumed

Power should enter through explicit capabilities rather than ambient access. Public teaching can frame this as a stewardship principle: an agent, worker, or system should receive only the powers required for its bounded role, and denied capabilities should remain visible rather than silently bypassed.

### Composition is relational

Unit's public graph model supports a safe derived metaphor for IAM: complex behavior emerges through relationships among bounded units, inputs, outputs, and transformations. The graph is a map of dependency and exchange, not proof of a cosmological claim.

### Mythic doctrine does not control runtime

Repository boundaries explicitly place doctrine and speculative architecture outside executable kernel authority. VisionaryBrains should preserve this distinction: mythic language may orient meaning and values, but evidence, policy, contracts, and explicit grants govern actual operations.

## Unsafe exclusions

- No private repository locations or internal retrieval links.

- No claim that IAM is conscious, divine, scientifically proven, or religiously binding.
- No claim that metaphors confer technical authority.
- No publication of PBOS governance mechanics.

## Teaching potential

1. **Who am I?** Identity as continuity across contexts.
2. **What may I do?** Capability as bounded permission.
3. **What carries the work?** Execution substrate as distinct from identity and policy.
4. **How do parts become a system?** Graph composition as relationship and transformation.
5. **What does the mythic layer do?** Meaning and orientation without overriding evidence.
