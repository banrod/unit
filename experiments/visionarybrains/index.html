What IAM means here

IAM is presented as a design and meaning framework. It asks how purpose remains coherent, how action receives permission, how relationships stay inspectable, and how metaphor can enrich interpretation without replacing evidence.

03

Nature

A governed ecology of responsibilities

IAM is taught here as coordinated specialization: bounded roles contribute different kinds of work through explicit handoffs, evidence, permissions, review, and challenge paths.

Change should remain repairable

Claims should show their evidence and authority; consequential changes should be reviewable; uncertainty should stay visible; and correction, amendment, contest, and recovery should remain possible.

04

Deepening

Theology as optional meaning language

Here, theology names optional mythic language used to explore purpose, relationship, memory, return, and stewardship.

∅

Metaphor is invitation, not authority

Mythic and cosmological language is optional. It does not establish scientific proof, religious obligation, unrestricted technical access, operational authority, medical or psychological judgment, or privileged claims about another person's identity.
